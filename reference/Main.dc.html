Sound familiar?

Most “problem dogs” just haven’t been shown another way yet.

Your dog lunges and barks when another dog walks by. Every walk turns into a tug-of-war. They panic when you pick up your keys, jump on every guest, or treat your hands like chew toys.

None of that makes them a bad dog — or you a bad owner. These are some of the most common things I help with, and they can all get better with the right plan and steady practice.
