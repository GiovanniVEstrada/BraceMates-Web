Training philosophy

How I train

  • Behavior is communication

    Barking, pulling and jumping all mean something. We figure out the why first, so the fix actually sticks.

  • Teach, don’t punish

    I use reward-based methods [confirm] to show your dog what to do instead. It’s kinder, and it builds trust between you.

  • You’re half the team

    I coach you as much as your dog, with skills that fit your real routine — so progress keeps going between visits.

  • Real life, real places

    We train in your home and on your streets — where the problems actually happen.
